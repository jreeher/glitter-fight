1 Overview

Smashy is marching towards the Cloud Kingdom! Players must use their memory and the magic tiles on the board to defeat Smashy before he reaches the castle.

This is a cooperative memory matching game for 1–4 players — all players are on the same team and work together to defeat Smashy. Each round, Smashy rolls the dice and takes his turn first — then the player flips two tiles to find matching pairs. Each matched pair triggers a powerful ability!

3 Setup
  • 1 Firsties: The player who has most recently touched glitter goes first, followed by the player to their left.
  • 2 Choose a difficulty: Easy (blue dice), Medium (purple dice), Hard (yellow dice), or Ultra Smash Mode (yellow dice, 15 HP).
  • 3 Shuffle all selected tiles face-down and lay them out in a winding path on the table (can be messy or ordered, as long as you know the path!). Place Smashy's Mountain face-up at the start of the path and the Rainbow Gate face-up at the end of the path, closest to the castle.
  • 4 Set up the castle: Place the castle at the far end of the tile path. Place the shield, freeze, and gelatinous cube tokens beside the castle.
  • 5 Place Smashy's token on the farthest tile from the castle. Place the rock tokens beside Smashy.
  • 6 Set Smashy's HP: Easy/Medium/Hard — 12 health tokens. Ultra Smash Mode — add the 3 extra health tokens (15 HP).
4 How to Play

Turn Order: Smashy always goes first — players roll the dice for Smashy's action. After Smashy's turn ends, the player flips two tiles over. If they match, the power activates! If not, they are flipped back over.

Empty Spaces: Smashy only steps on unmatched tiles — so with every matched tile, his path to the castle gets smaller.

To Start Every Turn — Smashy Rolls the Dice
See Section 6 for what each dice face does.
The Player's Turn — Flip Two Tiles
Flip any face-down tile, then flip a second tile. Two outcomes are possible:

Match! Both tiles show the same image. The power activates immediately, and the pair is removed from play (left face-up, out of the game).

No Match. Both tiles are flipped back over. Try to remember where they were!
5 Tile Powers

When you match a pair, the tile's power triggers immediately. Each tile type is described below with its default number of pairs in a standard game.

Basic Tiles
All basic tiles either cause Smashy damage or push him back. The tile will say on the face what the action is and how much damage or pushback each one causes.
Feather Arrow
Smashy loses 1 health.
Cumulus Cannon
Smashy loses 2 health.
Rain-Bow
Smashy loses 3 health.
Cloudy
Smashy is pushed back 1 tile.
Unicorn Horn
Smashy loses 2 health and is pushed back 2 tiles.
Rainbow Tornado
Smashy is pushed back 3 tiles.
Special Tiles
Rainbow Royals
1 pair (special)
Smashy loses 3 health and is pushed back 3 tiles. This is not a typical match — the King tile and Queen tile look different but must be matched with each other.
Freeze Wand
Smashy loses 1 health. Flip over two tiles and place a freeze token on top of each — the tiles remain face up until a match is found.
Glitter Guard
Smashy loses 1 health. Place a shield token in front of the castle — if Smashy would enter the castle he is stopped and the shield is removed.
Gelatinous Cube
Smashy loses 1 health. Place a gelatinous cube token on any tile. If Smashy ends his turn on that tile, he is stuck in place for a turn and does not roll. If the tile below the cube is matched, the cube is removed from the game.
Sparkler
Flip over up to 4 tiles to peek at them — any tiles that are flipped this way are NOT considered matched.
Glitter Hole
Smashy loses 1 health. All rocks are immediately removed from the board.
6 Smashy's Turn

At the start of every turn, roll Smashy's die. There are 6 possible dice rolls. Smashy moves forward one tile for every foot shown on the die face. If Smashy is stuck on a tile with the gelatinous cube token, he does not roll that turn.

Die Face Effect
Step 1 Smashy moves forward 1 tile along the path (one tile for every foot shown on the die face).
Step 2 Smashy moves forward 2 tiles.
Step 3 Smashy moves forward 3 tiles.
Step 4 Smashy moves forward 4 tiles. Hard mode only!
Rock A rock is placed on the tile in front of Smashy (multiple rocks can be on the same tile). That tile cannot be flipped until the rock is cleared — match a Glitter Hole pair to remove all rocks instantly.
Thunder All tiles must be connected again to form a continuous path to the castle — slide the remaining tiles together to close any gaps left by matched tiles.
Taunt Nothing! Smashy taunts the players and does nothing this turn. Thankfully, only your pride is hurt!

8 Special Rules
Uh Oh!
If no tiles are available, the player must pass on their turn and Smashy rolls again.
Rainbow Gate
If Smashy throws a rock while standing on the Rainbow Gate, the rock is placed on the Rainbow Gate, since there are no tiles ahead of him.
Frozen Tiles & Rocks
If a rock is on a frozen tile, the tile can still be matched — and when it is, the rock is removed.
9 Winning & Losing
Victory!
Players win by removing all of Smashy's health.
Defeated!
Players lose if Smashy reaches the Cloud Castle (a shield token from the Glitter Guard tile can stop him once).
