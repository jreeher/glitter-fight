1 Overview

Smashy is marching towards the Cloud Kingdom! Players must use their memory and the magic tiles on the board to defeat Smashy before he reaches the castle.

This is a cooperative memory matching game for 1–4 players. Flip tiles two at a time to find matching pairs — each matched pair triggers a powerful ability. But every time you flip tiles, Smashy takes a turn and advances along the path!

Players
1–4 players, cooperative. Works great solo too!

15–30 minutes

3 Setup
  • 1 Choose a difficulty: Easy (blue dice), Medium (purple dice), or Hard (yellow dice).
  • 2 Shuffle all selected tiles face-down and lay them out in a winding path on the table (can be messy or ordered, as long as you know the path!).
  • 3 Set up the castle: Place the castle at the far end of the tile path. Place the Glitter Guard and Fluff Wall tokens beside the castle.
  • 4 Place Smashy's token on the farthest tile from the castle. Place the rock tokens beside Smashy.
  • 5 Set Smashy's HP: For Easy and Medium difficulty, use all of the black cubes - Smashy will have a starting HP of 12. For Hard difficulty, you will add the 3 yellow cubes for a total HP of 15.
4 How to Play

Players alternate between Smashy's Turn and the Player's Turn. Smashy always goes first.

When Smashy moves, he skips over matched tiles that have been removed from the game — they don't count as steps. He only lands on unmatched tile spaces.

To Start Every Turn — Smashy Rolls the Dice
See Section 6 for what each dice face does.
The Player's Turn — Flip Two Tiles
Flip any face-down tile, then flip a second tile. Two outcomes are possible:

Match! Both tiles show the same image. Their power activates immediately, and the pair is removed from play (left face-up, out of the game).

No Match. Both tiles flip back face-down. Try to remember where they were!
5 Tile Powers

When you match a pair, the tile's power triggers immediately. Each tile type is described below with its default number of pairs in a standard game.

Basic Tiles
All basic tiles either cause Smashy damage or push him back. The tile will say on the face what the action is and how much damage or pushback each one causes.

Special Tiles
Rainbow Royals
1 pair (special)
Deals 3 damage to Smashy and pushes him back 3 spaces. This is not a typical match — the King tile and Queen tile look different but must be matched with each other.
Freeze Wand
1 pair
Deals 1 damage and Freezes Smashy for one turn — he skips his next dice roll entirely.
Glitter Guard
1 pair
Deals 1 damage to Smashy. After matching, place the Glitter Guard token between any two tiles on the board. If Smashy would pass that spot, he stops on the tile before it. The Glitter Guard is removed from play after stopping Smashy.
Wall of Fluff
1 pair
Adds 1 Wall of Fluff token to the Cloud Kingdom. Each token absorbs one breach when Smashy would hit the castle.
Sparkle Wand
1 pair
Lets you peek at up to 4 face-down tiles of your choice without flipping them. Peeked tiles are NOT considered matched and are not removed from play.
Glitter Hole
1 pair
When matched, immediately removes all rock tokens from the board and deal 2 damage to Smashy.
6 Smashy's Turn

At the start of every turn, roll Smashy's die. There are 6 possible dice rolls. If Smashy is Frozen , he skips his turn and the freeze is removed.

Die Face Effect
Charge 1 Smashy moves forward 1 tile along the path.
Charge 2 Smashy moves forward 2 tiles.
Charge 3 Smashy moves forward 3 tiles.
Charge 4 Smashy moves forward 4 tiles. Hard mode only!
Rock Smashy drops a rock token on an unmatched tile ahead. That tile cannot be flipped until the rock is moved or a Glitter Hole is matched. Max 2 rocks on the board at once — the oldest rock moves when a third would be placed. If no valid tile exists ahead, the oldest rock is removed permanently (falls off the map).
Earthquake All unmatched tiles condense towards the castle, sliding to fill any gaps left by matched tiles. Tile order stays the same — they just close the gaps.
Taunt Smashy taunts the players and does nothing this turn. Thankfully, only your pride is hurt!
7 Dice Guide
Easy Die (Blue)
Taunt ×2 · Step 1 ×2 · Step 2 ×2
Medium Die (Purple)
Step 1 · Step 2 · Step 3 · Rock ×2 · Earthquake
Hard Die (Yellow)
Step 1 · Step 2 · Step 3 · Step 4 · Rock · Earthquake
8 Special Rules
All Tiles Blocked
If every remaining face-down tile is covered by a rock or wall, the player cannot flip any tiles. Smashy automatically takes another turn until at least one tile becomes flippable again.
9 Winning & Losing
Victory!
Players win if Smashy's HP reaches 0 from tile damage, or if all tile pairs are matched before Smashy breaks through the castle.
Defeated!
Players lose if Smashy reaches the castle and no Fluff Walls remain to stop him.
