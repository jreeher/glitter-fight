Tickle Fight!
A Memory Matching Adventure
Rulebook
1 Overview

Smashy is marching towards the Cloud Kingdom! Players must use their memory and the magic tiles on the board to deal damage, push Smashy back, and defend the castle before he reches the castle.

This is a cooperative memory matching game for 1–4 players. Flip tiles two at a time to find matching pairs — each matched pair triggers a powerful ability. But every time you flip tiles, Smashy takes a turn and advances along the path!

Players
1–4 players, cooperative. Works great solo too!
Play Time
15–30 minutes

3 Setup
  • 1 Choose a difficulty: Easy (blue dice), Medium (purple dice), or Hard (yellow dice).
  • 2 Choose which tile pairs to include. For your first game, we recommend taking out the Glitter Hole, Cloud Guard, and Star Wand tiles.
  • 3 Shuffle all selected tiles face-down and lay them out in a winding path on the table.
  • 3.1 Set up the castle: Place the castle at the far end of the tile path. Place one Fluff Wall in front of the castle. Place any cloud tokens that match the chosen tiles (Glitter Hole, Fluff Wall, Cloud Guard) beside the castle.
  • 4 Place Smashy's token on the farthest tile from the castle. Place the rock tokens beside Smashy.
  • 5 Set Smashy's HP: With all tiles in play, Smashy has a maximum starting HP of 14. If you remove any damage causing tiles, also remove that many HP cubes from Smashy's card. For easy and medium difficulty, remove an additional 3 HP cubes before the game begins.
✦ ✦ ✦
4 How to Play

Players alternate between Smashy's Turn and the Player's Turn. Smashy always goes first.

To Start Every Turn — Smashy Rolls the Dice
See Section 6 for what each dice face does.
The Player's Turn — Flip Two Tiles
Flip any face-down tile, then flip a second tile. Two outcomes are possible:

Match! Both tiles show the same image. Their power activates immediately, and the pair is removed from play (left face-up, out of the game).

No Match. Both tiles flip back face-down. Try to remember where they were!
✦ ✦ ✦
5 Tile Powers

When you match a pair, the tile's power triggers immediately. Each tile type is described below with its default number of pairs in a standard game.

Basic Tiles
All basic tiles either cause Smashy damage or push him back. The tile will say on the face what the action is and how much damage or pushback each one causes.

Deals 1 damage to Smashy.

2 pairs
Deals 2 damage to Smashy.

1 pair
Deals 3 damage to Smashy.
Wind Blast
1 pair
Pushes Smashy back 1 space along the path.
Unicorn Horn
1 pair
Pushes Smashy back 2 spaces.
Rainbow Tornado
1 pair
Pushes Smashy back 3 spaces.
Special Tiles
Rainbow Royals
1 pair (special)
Deals 3 damage to Smashy and pushes him back 3 spaces. This is not a typical match — the King tile and Queen tile look different but must be matched with each other.
Freeze Wand
1 pair
Freezes Smashy for one turn — he skips his next dice roll entirely.
Cloud Guard
1 pair
After matching, place the Cloud Guard token in front of any tile on the board. If Smashy would pass that tile, hestops on the tile before it. The cloud guard is removed from play once Smashy reaches it.
Wall of Fluff
1 pair
Adds 1 Wall of Fluff token to the Cloud Kingdom. Each token absorbs one breach when Smashy or a rock would hit the castle.
Star Wand
1 pair
Lets you peek at up to 4 face-down tiles of your choice without flipping them. Peeked tiles are NOT considered matched and are not removed from play.
Glitter Hole
1 pair
After matching, place the Glitter Hole token in front of you. At any point during your turn, activate the Glitter Hole tile to remove all rock tokens from the board.

6 Smashy's Turn

At the start of every turn, roll Smashy's die. There are 6 possible dice rolls. If Smashy is Frozen , he skips his turn and the freeze is removed.

Die Face Effect
Charge 1 Smashy moves forward 1 tile along the path.
Charge 2 Smashy moves forward 2 tiles.
Charge 3 Smashy moves forward 3 tiles.
Charge 4 Smashy moves forward 4 tiles. Hard mode only!
Rock Smashy drops a rock token on an unmatched tile ahead. That tile cannot be flipped until the rock is moved by Smashy or removed by a Glitter Hole. Max 3 rocks on the board at once — the oldest rock moves once a fourth would be placed. If no tiles are ahead, the rock hits the castle and removes a Fluff Wall instead!
Swap Smashy swaps the next available face-down tile in front and behind him. If no tiles are behind, he swaps two ahead. If no tiles are ahead, he swaps two behind. If no swap is possible, the turn passes to the player.
Taunt Smashy taunts the players and does nothing this turn. Thankfully, only your pride is hurt!
Castle Breaches
Whenever Smashy reaches the end of the path (or a rock hits the castle), one Fluff Wall is removed. If a rock hits the castle but no shields remain, nothing happens and play continues. If Smashy himself enters the castle with no shields left, the players lose immediately!
✦ ✦ ✦
7 Special Rules
All Tiles Blocked
If every remaining face-down tile is covered by a rock or wall, the player cannot flip any tiles. Smashy automatically takes another turn until at least one tile becomes flippable again.
✦ ✦ ✦
8 Winning & Losing
Victory!
Players win if Smashy's HP reaches 0 from tile damage, or if all tile pairs are matched before Smashy breaks through the castle.
Defeated!
Players lose if Smashy reaches the castle and no Fluff Walls remain to stop him.
✦ ✦ ✦
9 Difficulty Guide
Easy
Die: Taunt, Taunt, Step 1, Step 1, Step 2, Step 2. Remove 3 health from Smashy's maximum health. Remove Glitter Hole, Cloud Guard, and Star Wand tiles.
Medium
Die: Step 1, Step 2, Step 3, Rock, Rock, Swap. Remove 3 health from Smashy's maximum health. All tiles in play.
Hard
Die: Step 1, Step 2, Step 3, Step 4, Rock, Swap. Smashy starts at full health. Only one pair of each tile allowed in play. Good luck!
Custom
Use whatever dice, health, and tiles you want. Who are we to tell you how to live your life?
